Workflow Illustration

The ticket workflow consolidates support requests that used to arrive through mixed informal channels. Intake, categorization, assignment, progress tracking, resolution, and closure become easier to monitor when every request moves through one defined service process.

Business Problem

Support requests become harder to triage and monitor when intake and status updates are spread across informal channels such as chat, calls, and direct messages.

My Contribution

Built the ticket workflow structure and tracking interface, plus supporting features for a clearer internal IT service process and better request ownership visibility.

Operational Flow

  • Requests are captured through one intake workflow instead of scattered manual follow-up.
  • Tickets are categorized and assigned to the appropriate support handler.
  • Progress status and action history improve visibility during troubleshooting and resolution.
  • Closure provides clearer records for users and IT support teams.

Key Capabilities

  • Structured request intake and ticket categorization.
  • Assignment and work-progress monitoring.
  • Support status visibility for users and IT teams.
  • Resolution and closure records for internal service review.

Tech Stack

PHP, Tailwind CSS, Python, Flask, OpenCV.
