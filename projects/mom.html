Mom (Minute Of Meeting Online)

Meeting workflow platform for scheduling, documenting outcomes, and tracking internal follow-up actions.

Private Repository Enterprise Workflow

Business Problem

Meeting decisions and follow-up tasks easily fragment across chat, spreadsheets, and manual notes. This platform centralizes the lifecycle of documenting and reviewing meeting outcomes.

My Contribution

Built the structured workflow and interface for meeting scheduling, minute capture, and follow-up visibility using Laravel, React, Inertia.js, and MySQL.

Stack: Laravel, React, Inertia.js, MySQL, Tailwind CSS.
