Digitalization Context

IK Digital in this context is a shopfloor digital work instruction system for final inspection quality stations. The improvement is operational and very specific: paper work instruction sheets, handwritten defect notes, and manual reference lookup at the station are replaced by one centralized digital instruction flow that appears after the operator scans the part tag from the pallet or box in queue.

Workflow Illustration

The shopfloor usage flow starts when pallets or boxes of parts arrive in queue at the final inspection station. The operator scans the part tag, the system loads the correct digital instruction, a 100 percent inspection video appears on screen, historical defect references are shown, and the operator completes the inspection before recording OK or NG for the next process decision.

Business Problem

Final inspection execution becomes inconsistent when operators still depend on printed work instructions, scattered defect examples, and manual reference checking at the station while parts keep arriving in queue from shopfloor production.

My Contribution

Structured the digital work instruction flow for final inspection so operators can scan part tags from boxes or pallets, load the correct instruction instantly, review 100 percent inspection video, and reference recurring defect history directly on the station screen.

Operational Flow

  • Boxes or pallets of parts arrive in queue at the final inspection quality station.
  • The operator scans the part tag on the pallet or box before starting inspection.
  • The correct digital work instruction loads automatically on the station display.
  • A 100 percent inspection video and defect checklist guide the checking process.
  • Historical defect references help the operator focus on recurring issues that have appeared before.
  • The operator records OK or NG and then continues to the next part in queue.

Key Capabilities

  • Scan-based loading of digital work instruction by part tag.
  • Centralized station display for inspection steps and visual guidance.
  • 100 percent inspection video support directly at final inspection.
  • Historical defect list and checklist visibility for recurring quality issues.
  • More consistent instruction access than paper-based station references.

Tech Stack

WP Framework, PHP, MySQL.
