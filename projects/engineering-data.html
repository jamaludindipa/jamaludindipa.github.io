Internal engineering reference platform for centralized document access and operational lookup.

Internal Platform Engineering Reference Document Lookup

Workflow Illustration

This platform centralizes engineering references that would otherwise live across folders, paper prints, email attachments, and inconsistent file structures. The focus is helping engineering users find drawings, specifications, and controlled references faster with better confidence in revision and document status.

Business Problem

Engineering references become harder to retrieve when documents and operational information are scattered across unmanaged storage and unclear access patterns.

My Contribution

Built the UI structure and access flow for centralizing engineering information into a more usable internal reference platform with better lookup and visibility.

Operational Flow

  • Technical references are gathered into one platform instead of fragmented storage locations.
  • Documents can be categorized and searched more consistently by engineering users.
  • Revision and status visibility reduce the risk of using outdated references.
  • Operational lookup becomes faster during daily engineering work.

Key Capabilities

  • Centralized engineering document and reference access.
  • Search and categorization flow for faster lookup.
  • Reference visibility for drawings, specs, and related files.
  • Improved usability for day-to-day engineering retrieval.

Tech Stack

PHP, MySQL, Tailwind CSS.

Current Status

Status: Internal reference platform focused on faster engineering lookup, clearer organization, and more dependable access to shared technical information.
