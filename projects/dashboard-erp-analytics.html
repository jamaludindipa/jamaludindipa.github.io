Dashboard ERP Analytics

A consolidated operational analytics portal for PPIC, Management, and IT, bringing ERP-synchronized KPIs, visual trends, drill-down detail, and export workflows into one departmental dashboard environment.

Business Problem

Operational analysis becomes slow and inconsistent when each department relies on separate reports, manual recaps, and disconnected dashboard views. Decision makers need a shared portal that preserves department context while making KPI status, trend analysis, and detailed records easier to access.

My Role & Ownership

Independently designed and built the portal structure, departmental navigation, dashboard presentation, filter and drill-down interactions, ERP synchronization flow, reporting views, and responsive interface as one end-to-end internal analytics solution.

Department Coverage

PPIC

Vendor claims, stock, production planning, material receipt, purchase orders, supplier performance, capacity, shortage risk, inventory aging, and action monitoring.

Management

Customer delivery status, customer claims, efficiency by line, daily efficiency trends, and detailed delivery analysis.

IT

Checklist schedule compliance, approval signatures, staff compliance, active templates, exceptions, overdue schedules, and corrective actions.

Key Capabilities

  • Department-oriented navigation for PPIC, Management, and IT analytics.
  • KPI cards, charts, status thresholds, and trend views for operational monitoring.
  • Period, organization, vendor, customer, plant, location, product, and category filters where relevant.
  • Drill-down views that connect summary indicators to detailed operational records.
  • ERP data synchronization and Excel export actions within the dashboard workflow.
  • User, role, audit, dashboard configuration, and database administration entry points.

Tech Stack

Laravel, React, Tailwind CSS, and MySQL.

Current Status

Internal MVP focused on consolidating departmental analysis, strengthening reusable dashboard patterns, and improving access from summary KPIs to actionable detail.
