One System, Two Operational Interfaces

The go-live solution provides a kiosk designed for direct shopfloor actions and a management portal for planning, control, approval, monitoring, and analysis. Both interfaces work with the same internal maintenance data so operational execution and management visibility remain connected.

Business Problem

Maintenance operations require more than a Work Order list. Machine issues, preventive schedules, technician assignment, parts availability, approval, verification, shift continuity, and performance reporting must stay connected so the team can act quickly and management can evaluate reliability from consistent operational records.

My Role & Ownership

Independently analyzed the maintenance workflow and delivered the application end to end: solution structure, management portal, Vue SPA interactions, shopfloor kiosk, station and employee PIN flow, Work Order lifecycle, inventory views, reporting experience, PostgreSQL persistence, testing, Linux deployment, and go-live improvement.

End-to-End Maintenance Workflow

  1. 1. Report / PM DueIssue or scheduled maintenance enters the operational flow.
  2. 2. Work OrderThe system records machine, type, priority, scope, and status.
  3. 3. Approval & AssignmentA supervisor reviews the WO and assigns the maintenance team.
  4. 4. Employee PINThe technician accesses protected kiosk actions at the selected station.
  5. 5. Repair / PM & PartsExecution, notes, timing, and internal spare-part usage are recorded.
  6. 6. VerificationThe completed work and machine condition are reviewed.
  7. 7. Closure & HandoverThe WO closes and relevant shift continuity is preserved.
  8. 8. Performance ReportingDashboards expose reliability, backlog, downtime, and compliance.
