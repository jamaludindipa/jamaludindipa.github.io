HRMS-HRIS

Internal human resource workflow platform for employee data management, attendance monitoring, leave requests, payroll support, and approval process visibility.

Internal MVP HR Workflow Sanitized Screenshots

Business Problem

HR records, attendance checks, leave requests, payroll preparation, and approval routing lose efficiency when they are handled across separate files or disconnected operational steps. HRMS-HRIS was built to bring those workflows into one internal system with clearer status visibility.

My Contribution

Built the internal workflow structure and application UI for organizing employee administration, attendance visibility, request handling, and approval-oriented HR processes in a more operationally usable format.

Key Capabilities

  • Centralized employee profile and administration workflow.
  • Attendance and leave visibility with status-oriented handling.
  • Payroll-support flow within the same internal HR context.
  • Approval workflow structure for day-to-day HR operations.

Tech Stack

Laravel, PHP, MySQL, Tailwind CSS.

Privacy note: screenshots and data shown are sanitized. Internal employee data, credentials, endpoints, and private company information are excluded.
